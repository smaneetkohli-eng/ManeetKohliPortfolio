Hello, I'm Maneet. I study Business Analytics and Artificial Intelligence.

I don't see AI as a substitute for the human mind. I see it as a way to clear the noise around it: the repetition, administration, and fragmented execution that quietly consume our attention. The point is not to think less. It is to think more deeply about the problems worth solving.

That is what I explore through products, businesses, and videos. I want to build things that help people reclaim their attention, make better decisions, and turn ideas into work that matters.

AI can carry more of the load. But people still choose the direction.

Projects and Experiences

Own the company.
